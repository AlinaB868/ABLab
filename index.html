02 / ABOUT ME

Curiosity is where it starts.

My background is in Environmental Engineering, Biotechnology, and more than a decade of work on European-funded projects. Curiosity eventually led me toward a different kind of problem-solving: building and testing software.

I originally wanted to study cybersecurity, but a frontend course gave me an unexpected starting point. Learning how web applications are built helped me ask better questions about how they work, where they can fail, and how to test them.

That connection drew me toward web security. I learn by experimenting: setting up an Ubuntu virtual machine, exploring security tools, writing code, making mistakes, and finding out why something behaves the way it does.

OR2EQ1 began as a handful of practical exercises to demonstrate my ICT skills. One question led to another, and those exercises grew into an interactive cybersecurity learning project. Alongside it, I build websites for clients and explore AI security and human oversight through prototypes.

I use AI assistants as part of my workflow, together with documentation, hands-on testing, and critical review. I'm still learning—and I want the projects here to show what I've actually built, explored, and understood.
